## Two new templates

**astro-component-kit 0.6.0** adds two complete, multi-page templates and **34 reusable sections**, bringing the catalog to **5 templates**.

### 📣 Volta: marketing agency

An 8-page site for an agency selling **Paid Ads, SEO and web development**: home, services, service detail, case studies, case study, pricing, about and contact. Dark by default with a light theme, a kinetic hero, an ROI calculator, a free-audit CTA and pricing packages.

- 19 sections in **Sections → Agency & Marketing**
- Live preview: [/preview/agency/](/preview/agency/)

### ⌨️ Keycap: developer portfolio

A monochrome, keyboard-inspired personal site with six pages (home, projects, case study, blog, article and contact) and real **3D sections**:

- A projects showcase whose cards animate code, terminals or a paint canvas from your data
- A mechanical keyboard that types your stack
- A GitHub-style contribution matrix whose cubes fly in and ripple like water
- A CRT monitor and a breathing dot-matrix hero

The 3D scenes follow the light/dark theme, pause off-screen and respect reduced motion. They need the optional `three` peer dependency.

- 15 sections in **Sections → Developer Portfolio**
- Live preview: [/preview/portfolio/](/preview/portfolio/)
