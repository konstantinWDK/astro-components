## Veritas University: 5 pages and 9 dedicated components for higher education institutions

Veritas University (`templates/university`) is an institutional multi-page template crafted specifically for modern universities, colleges of higher education, and research academies. Built with a scholarly aesthetic combining deep midnight blue, academic gold, and crisp typography, it delivers the complete student and prospective applicant journey.

---

### Veritas · University & Higher Education Campus (`templates/university`)

- **Live preview**: [/preview/university/](/preview/university/) · **Template docs**: [/templates/university/](/templates/university/)
- **Institutional Hero**: International prestige badge (#1 Forbes Employability), distinguished serif headline, interactive degree finder (level and faculty selects), and campus KPIs.
- **Degree & Program Directory**: Full catalog with level tabs (Bachelors, Masters, Doctorates, Executive), faculty filter chips, instant search, ECTS credits, language badges, and career outlet tags.
- **Admissions Journey**: 4-step roadmap with requirements checklist and official deadlines calendar table for admission rounds.
- **Interactive Tuition & Scholarship Calculator**: Real-time simulation of official tuition, merit scholarship discounts (15%, 30%, 50%), campus residence and athletic center add-ons, and a 10-installment monthly payment plan with application modal.
- **Scientific Research Institutes**: Showcase of 42 research groups with 54M € funding metrics, Nature/IEEE publication highlights, and corporate R&D partnership callout.
- **Campus Life & Facilities**: 24/7 central library, prototyping FabLab, student residence hall, sports complex, student clubs, and international mobility agreements.
- **Open Days Registration**: Interactive session cards (in-person vs streaming) with registration form and instant QR access pass booking confirmation dialog.
- **5 Synchronized Pages**: [Home](/preview/university/) · [Programs](/preview/university/programas/) · [Admissions](/preview/university/admisiones/) · [Research](/preview/university/investigacion/) · [Campus](/preview/university/campus/).
- **9 Dedicated Components**: [`UnivHeader`](/docs/univ-header/), [`UnivFooter`](/docs/univ-footer/), [`UnivHero`](/docs/univ-hero/), [`UnivPrograms`](/docs/univ-programs/), [`UnivAdmissions`](/docs/univ-admissions/), [`UnivCalculator`](/docs/univ-calculator/), [`UnivResearch`](/docs/univ-research/), [`UnivCampus`](/docs/univ-campus/), [`UnivOpenDay`](/docs/univ-open-day/).
