## Lumen: learn-by-doing course platform, in nine pages

Coming in the next release. **Lumen** (`templates/academy`) is a complete template for an online academy, bootcamp or course marketplace. It has its own ink-blue and amber "reading lamp" visual language with a serif display face, and it is built only from its own dedicated components (`reusedComponents: []`).

---

### 💡 Lumen · Online Academy, Learner Area & Instructor Studio (`templates/academy`)

- **Live preview**: [/preview/academy/](/preview/academy/) · **Template docs**: [/templates/academy/](/templates/academy/)
- **Public site (5 pages)**: home with course search, learning paths, featured courses, instructors, reviews and plans; a catalog that filters by search, category, level and sorting (and opens pre-filtered via `?q=` and `?cat=`); a course page with a sticky enrolment card and syllabus accordion; plans with a monthly/yearly switch and FAQ; and an instructor application form.
- **Learner area & studio (4 pages)**: sign-in with a learner/instructor switch, a learner dashboard (weekly-goal ring, study streak, courses in progress), a lesson player with scrubber, speed, per-lesson completion and notes saved in the browser plus a quiz, and an instructor studio with KPIs, a 12-month chart and a sortable course manager.
- **Accessible by default**: native `<details>` and popovers, text alternatives for every chart and ring, 44px targets, visible focus rings and reduced-motion support. The theme has dark and light variants.
- **Pages**: [Home](/preview/academy/) · [Catalog](/preview/academy/cursos/) · [Course page](/preview/academy/cursos/diseno-de-interfaces-desde-cero/) · [Plans](/preview/academy/precios/) · [Teach](/preview/academy/ensena/) · [Sign in](/preview/academy/acceso/) · [Learner dashboard](/preview/academy/aula/) · [Lesson player](/preview/academy/aula/clase/) · [Instructor studio](/preview/academy/aula/estudio/).
- **19 dedicated components**: [`AcademyHero`](/sections/academy-hero/), [`AcademyTracks`](/sections/academy-tracks/), [`AcademyCourseGrid`](/sections/academy-course-grid/), [`AcademyCourseHero`](/sections/academy-course-hero/), [`AcademyCurriculum`](/sections/academy-curriculum/), [`AcademyPricingPlans`](/sections/academy-pricing-plans/), [`AcademyInstructors`](/sections/academy-instructors/), [`AcademyReviews`](/sections/academy-reviews/), [`AcademyTeachForm`](/sections/academy-teach-form/), [`AcademyFooter`](/sections/academy-footer/), [`AcademyLessonPlayer`](/docs/academy-lesson-player/), [`AcademyProgressBoard`](/docs/academy-progress-board/), [`AcademyQuiz`](/docs/academy-quiz/), [`AcademyStudioStats`](/docs/academy-studio-stats/), [`AcademyCoursesTable`](/docs/academy-courses-table/), [`AcademyAreaNav`](/docs/academy-area-nav/), [`AcademyAreaTopbar`](/docs/academy-area-topbar/), [`AcademyAreaHeading`](/docs/academy-area-heading/), and [`AcademySignIn`](/docs/academy-sign-in/).
