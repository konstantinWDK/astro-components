## Pulse Tech Summit: 5 pages and 9 dedicated components for flagship developer conferences

Pulse Tech Summit (`templates/tech-event`) is designed for modern engineering summits, developer conferences, and tech conventions. Built around a high-contrast cyber-glow aesthetic with electric cyan and magenta accents, it provides complete attendee journeys from speaker exploration to schedule bookmarking and ticket checkout.

---

### Pulse · Tech Conference & Developer Summit (`templates/tech-event`)

- **Live preview**: [/preview/tech-event/](/preview/tech-event/) · **Template docs**: [/templates/tech-event/](/templates/tech-event/)
- **Live Countdown Hero**: Real-time ticker counting down to opening day, dynamic attendance statistics, and direct CTA actions.
- **Track Exploration**: 4 distinct tracks (AI & Autonomous Agents, Cloud Infrastructure, Developer Experience & Web, Security & Resilience) with themed iconography.
- **Interactive 3-Day Schedule**: Day selector tabs, track filter chips, real-time query search, and a personal agenda bookmarking toggle saved to `localStorage`.
- **Keynote & Speaker Directory**: Roster with track categorization, keynote designations, speaker bios, and social handles.
- **Dynamic Ticket Checkout**: Tiered passes (Virtual, In-Person Full Pass, Executive VIP), interactive ticket counter, promo code validator (`PULSE20`), automated 21% VAT computation, and accessible dialog modal.
- **Interactive SVG Venue Blueprint**: Interactive floor plan diagram with clickable stages (Main Arena, Hack Lab, Workshop Studio, Lounge), transportation guide, and discounted attendee hotel partners.
- **Sponsor & Partner Showcase**: Tiered brand showcase (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Community) with developer community outreach and sponsor inquiry modal.
- **5 Complete Pages**: [Home](/preview/tech-event/) · [Schedule](/preview/tech-event/programa/) · [Speakers](/preview/tech-event/ponentes/) · [Tickets](/preview/tech-event/entradas/) · [Venue](/preview/tech-event/sede/).
- **9 Dedicated Components**: [`PulseHeader`](/docs/pulse-header/), [`PulseFooter`](/docs/pulse-footer/), [`PulseHero`](/docs/pulse-hero/), [`PulseTracks`](/docs/pulse-tracks/), [`PulseSpeakers`](/docs/pulse-speakers/), [`PulseSchedule`](/docs/pulse-schedule/), [`PulseTickets`](/docs/pulse-tickets/), [`PulseVenue`](/docs/pulse-venue/), [`PulseSponsors`](/docs/pulse-sponsors/).
