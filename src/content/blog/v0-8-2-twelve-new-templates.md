## Twelve new templates, each with its own components

**astro-component-kit 0.8.2** adds twelve complete multi-page templates. Each one has its own look, its own components (`reusedComponents: []`), a live preview, light and dark themes, translated copy and an entry in the MCP server. They are UI with demo data: nothing is sent, charged or stored.
