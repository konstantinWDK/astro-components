## Cota: an architecture studio that draws its own plans, in eight pages

Cota (`templates/architecture`) is a complete template for an architecture studio. It has its own graphite and acid-lime visual language with a heavy grotesque (Archivo) and a mono (IBM Plex Mono) for measurements, no photography, no icon grids and no tag clouds: the generated drawings, the typography and the calculations carry it. It is built only from its own dedicated components (`reusedComponents: []`).

---

### Cota · Architecture Studio (`templates/architecture`)

- **Live preview**: [/preview/architecture/](/preview/architecture/) · **Template docs**: [/templates/architecture/](/templates/architecture/)
- **Public site (5 pages)**: a home whose hero draws a building elevation line by line, with blueprint dimension lines, recent work as an index with floor plans generated by binary space partition, an interactive solar study and the studio principles; the full built-work index with a type filter, plan, elevation and facts; the studio, with the process as a Gantt accordion; the estimate, with a cost calculator (price range, breakdown and a calendar of design, permit and works) and a request form; and client sign-in.
- **The solar study**: it computes the real altitude and azimuth of the sun for the studio's latitude. Move the hour, change the month or raise the neighbouring building to the south and watch the shadow fall on the house, with the percentage of lit facade and the hours of direct sun at 3 m written out.
- **Client area (3 pages)**: a work home with a huge progress figure beside an elevation built floor by floor as far as the progress, the phases, the team and what waits for an answer; a budget with paid-versus-budget bars per line, change orders to approve or reject with live totals, and pending decisions with the trade-off of each option in words; and documents with a status filter and signing plus a searchable site diary.
- **Accessible by default**: native range, radio and `<details>` controls, every number a drawing shows is also written as text, live regions for every change, 44–48px targets, visible focus, text equivalents for every drawing and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Dark and light variants.
